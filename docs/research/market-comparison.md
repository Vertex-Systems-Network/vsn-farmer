# Market Comparison
| Service | Region | What it does | Lesson for us |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plantix (PEAT) | Global, strong in India/PK | Photo diagnosis, community, input shop | Diagnosis is commodity → differentiate on Farm Memory + follow-up |
| Telenor/Jazz Khushaal Zamindar, Jazz Kissan | Pakistan | IVR/SMS weather + advisory via telco | Voice/IVR reach; low-literacy users |
| PxD (Precision Development) | PK, India, Africa | Free phone-based advisory, govt partnered | Govt/donor-funded model; A/B tested messages |
| Digital Green Farmer.Chat | India, Kenya, Ethiopia | GenAI RAG chatbot over extension content, voice | Closest to our idea — RAG + extension videos + voice |
| Kisan e-Mitra / KissanAI (Dhenu) | India | Govt AI chatbot; agri-tuned LLM | Fine-tuned small agri LLM to cut cost |
| DeHaat, AgroStar, BharatAgri | India | Advisory + input sales + market linkage | Revenue comes from inputs/output, not advice |
| Farmdar, Ricult, Agrimatics | Pakistan | Satellite insights, credit, B2B | B2B payers exist in PK |
| Cropin, Climate FieldView, xarvio | Global | Enterprise farm mgmt, satellite, prescriptions | Later Organization role / agri-business tier |
| Gooey.AI / AgriAssist, Ulangizi | Africa/global | WhatsApp GenAI farmer bots | WhatsApp-first is proven |

**Our edge (if done right):** Farm Memory + Crop Timeline + human-expert loop + follow-up outcomes data in Urdu → measurable advice quality, which most competitors lack.
**Better-feature ideas for banks:** WhatsApp/voice-first channel, mandi price + sell-timing, input-authenticity scan, dealer/agent network, outcome-verified advice ranking, B2B dashboards for lenders/insurers, offline PWA.


## Pakistan update — research 2026-10-22 (T-002)
The Pakistani market is **already crowded** with Urdu AI farm tools launched in 2025–26:
- Telenor **Kissan Dost Bashir** (Apr 2026): AI voice bot on 7272, mandi rates + weather advisory.
- **BaKhabar Kissan** (claimed 500k+ downloads): AI disease detection, satellite monitoring.
- **Cropwise Grower** (Syngenta): Crop Doctor AI, calendars, "Savera" female AI advisor.
- **KissanBot** (Sybrid AI), **Khaity** (7 languages claimed), **Kissan Madadgar** (Concave Agri).

**What this means for us:** "Urdu AI chatbot + photo diagnosis" alone is no longer unique. Our difference must be:
1. **Farm Memory + Crop Timeline** (advice knows your farm's history)
2. **Human expert check** on risky answers (trust)
3. **Follow-up + proven outcomes** (we can show advice worked)
4. **Brand-neutral** advice (Cropwise / input apps push their own products)
5. Voice + WhatsApp from early on (Telenor shows voice is what farmers use)

Details and sources: `banks/systems-bank.md`. Several PK claims are self-reported (blog / app-store); verify by installing the apps.
