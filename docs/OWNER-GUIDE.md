# Owner Guide (non-technical) — simple Roman Urdu

## Project kya hai
Ek app jo kisan ko Urdu me uski fasal ke baare me mashwara deti hai: sawal poochho, photo bhejo, mausam ka alert lo.
AI jawab deta hai, aur khatarnak sawal (jaise dawai ki miqdaar) pehle expert check karta hai.

## Kaam ki taqseem
| Kaun | Kya karta hai |
|---|---|
| **Aap (owner)** | Kisanon se baat, ijazat lena, experts dhoondna, accounts banwana, faisle approve karna |
| **AI (Claude, Cursor, Copilot, Gemini…)** | Saara technical kaam: research, tools chunna, code likhna, test karna |

## AI ko kaam kaise dena hai
Kisi bhi AI tool me ye copy-paste karen:

> AGENTS.md parho, phir tasks/CURRENT.md dekho aur agla task mukammal karo. Kaam ke baad CURRENT.md update karo aur commit karo. Mujhe aasan Urdu me batao ke kya kiya.

Bas. Har AI wahin se shuru kare ga jahan pichla ruka tha.

## Aap ki checklist
- [ ] **Kisanon se baat (10–15):** Faisalabad / Multan / Sheikhupura. Neeche sawal list hai.
- [ ] **1–2 agriculture officers** se milna (zila agriculture office).
- [ ] **Content ki ijazat:** Punjab Agriculture Department / AARI Faisalabad / PARC se likhit ijazat ke unki crop guides app me istemal hon.
- [ ] **2 experts:** ek agronomist, ek plant-protection (keera/bimari). Part-time bhi chalega.
- [ ] **Accounts (business ke naam par):** JazzCash + Easypaisa merchant account, WhatsApp Business number (alag SIM), company/NTN agar ho.
- [ ] **Budget** tay karna: AI aur server ka mahana kharcha (AI estimate de ga).

## Kisanon se poochne wale sawal
1. Aap kaun si fasal kitne acre par lagate hain?
2. Masla aaye (bimari, keera) to kis se poochte hain? Dealer, padosi, agri officer?
3. Smartphone kis ke paas hai? Aap khud chalate hain ya beta/beti?
4. WhatsApp istemal karte hain? Voice note bhejte hain?
5. Pichle saal sab se bara nuqsan kis cheez se hua?
6. Mausam ka alert kahan se milta hai?
7. Aisi app ke liye mahine ke kitne paise denge? (0 / 100 / 300 / 500)
8. Dawai/khaad ka dealer kitna bharosemand hai? Nakli dawai ka masla hai?
