# System: Digital Green Farmer.Chat
- Region / users: India, Kenya, Ethiopia, Nigeria; extension workers + farmers
- Business model: Donor / govt funded
- Status: partly-verified
- Sources (checked 2026-10-22): https://www.digitalgreen.org/insights/digital-green-openai-farmerchat ; https://openai.com/customer-stories/digital-green — 1M+ users (self-reported), knowledge base validated by Indian Ministry of Agriculture, used via extension agents

## Features
- GenAI RAG over vetted extension content & videos
- Voice in/out, WhatsApp/Telegram

## Strengths
- Closest to our concept; strong content partnerships

## Gaps / weaknesses
- Weak personal farm records; focused on extension workers

## Improve on it (ideas for us)
- Combine RAG with farm history + outcome tracking
- Urdu/Punjabi content partnerships (PARC, AARI)
